A personal email assistant

Useful email assistance.
Under the owner’s direction.

Hermes Default Gmail connects Mohamed Hagras’s personal assistant to his own Gmail account, to find messages, prepare responses and save drafts with attachments.

This is a private-use application for its owner. This information website does not provide mailbox access, a public sign-up service or a Google sign-in form.

Read and prepare

Search messages, read relevant email content and prepare drafts for review. Files selected by the owner can be attached to a draft.

Act with authorization

Gmail’s draft permission also permits sending. Saving a draft is a separate operation from sending. Sending is performed only within an owner-authorized task.

How it is used

The assistant runs in the owner’s existing environment and is used through the owner’s Telegram conversation. Google authorization is handled by Google. Credentials are held on the owner’s computer, separate from this public information site.

Understand the data flow

Email content needed for a task may be processed by the configured AI service, and requested results may be shown in Telegram. Local task history and prepared messages can be retained for continuity. Read the privacy policy and terms of use before connecting an account.
